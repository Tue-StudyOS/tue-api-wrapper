# TUE API App Store readiness review

**Release verdict: hold public submission.** The remaining gates are public service reachability, authorized reviewer access, publisher and privacy details, and distribution signing. The review started at `885e9f4`. The working tree removes client-side GitHub feedback and limits the app's Alma connection to the official HTTPS origin.

## Live service check on the Mac

With eduVPN connected, direct checks passed for Alma timetable, grades and document choices, Moodle deadlines and grades, ILIAS tasks, mail folder listing, Seatfinder and Talks. The Swift clients were exercised for Alma, Moodle, ILIAS, Seatfinder and Talks. The mail check used the local Python client. No university records were changed.

The public directory redirected to its maintenance page. The iOS app now reports maintenance instead of a missing search field. Catalogue, food, KuF Count and careers passed direct upstream checks, but all four configured Cloud Run routes still returned 404 while the VPN was connected. The app routes need a working deployment before release.

If included, pin and check the model revision and license, verify first-run download and low-memory behavior, and explain the network and storage use.
2. **Reconcile the privacy label with the specific release build.** The archive includes privacy manifests for the app, widget and Swift Crypto. Removing public feedback also removes its user content and diagnostics from the first-party manifest. Inventory each shipped network path, including university portals, public data, the hosted KuF count service, and any model download. ## Privacy policy draft for publisher review

TUE API lets you browse campus resources without a university login. If you connect your university account, the app stores your login in this device's Keychain. The app uses it to connect directly to university services and show your timetable, assignments, grades, documents and mail. The app keeps a copy of your timetable and assignments on the device so it can show them later and schedule reminders. The widget extension reads the timetable from storage shared with the app.

The app uses the camera when you scan a KuF or Deutschlandticket barcode. It saves scanned passes on the device. The campus map requests location access to show where you are. You can manage camera, location and notification permissions in iOS Settings.

Disconnect your university account in Account → Connections and reminders to remove the saved login, timetable and deadlines. Remove saved passes from their respective screens. Files you export through the iOS share sheet stay with the apps or storage locations you choose.

Before publication, add the publisher's legal name and contact, the final list of external services and their purposes, the hosted API's verified data retention, and how users can exercise privacy rights. Include model downloads if the Assistant ships. Publish the final policy at a stable HTTPS URL and link it in Account and App Store Connect.

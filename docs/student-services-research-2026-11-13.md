# University service discovery: digital student life

Research date: 13 November 2026. This is a source-led discovery list, not an
exhaustive inventory or a claim that any API or automation is authorized. The
linked service owner confirms the described user-facing service. Authentication,
machine access, terms, field coverage, and practical reliability still require
separate investigation. Priorities reflect likely agent usefulness, not a
completed implementation estimate.

These are external public authorities or providers, not university systems.
Use the resident's actual municipality and personal situation; city procedures
do not necessarily apply to students who live elsewhere. Provide official
sources and date checked, and do not treat automated guidance as a legal decision.

| Priority | Service and confirmed evidence | Candidate agent support and open questions |

## Network and operating constraints

The [ZDV Alma service page](https://uni-tuebingen.de/einrichtungen/zentrum-fuer-datenverarbeitung/dienstleistungen/server/alma-portal/) says external access requires university eduVPN. A [department notice dated 8 November 2026](https://uni-tuebingen.de/fakultaeten/mathematisch-naturwissenschaftliche-fakultaet/fachbereiche/informatik/lehrstuehle/neuronale-informationsverarbeitung/news/newsfullview-aktuell/article/aenderung-der-zugriffsmoeglichkeit-einzelner-systeme/) says some services, including ZDV webmail, are currently restricted to the university network or VPN. These are published operating statements, not a live test of every wrapper endpoint. A persistent agent needs to surface network and VPN failures distinctly from empty results.

## Next evidence-gathering steps

1. Compare the wrapper's SDK, HTTP routes, CLI and MCP tool list against each candidate, including field-level support and read/write behavior.
2. For library account and Alma payments, inspect user-visible pages with the account owner's authorization. Verify authentication, actual fields and error states before design or scraping.
3. Select representative ILIAS and Moodle courses to verify whether assignments, attachments, announcements and submission status are actually complete.
4. Build an inventory of official faculty-specific exam rules and module-handbook sources, including versions and effective dates.
5. Prioritize sources with a reliable supported feed or interface and a clear agent use case. Investigate operator rules before automating access.
